A Kid's Guide to the Big Song • Les Misérables

One Day More!

Paris, 1832. Tomorrow is the most dangerous and exciting day of their lives! Here is how each character steps forward before the giant finish:

0:00 – 0:34

A Dad on the Run

Jean Valjean sings alone with slow, heavy marching music as he prepares to flee Paris.

0:35 – 1:17

Young Sweethearts

Marius and Cosette fall in love while loyal Éponine watches sadly from the rainy street.

1:18 – 2:26

Friends, Spies & Foes

Enjolras rallies student rebels, Javert plots his spy mission, and thieves sneak below.

2:27 – 3:40

The Giant Climax

All 9 vocal lines sing at the exact same moment to shout one huge, unforgettable final note!

Auto-Sync is ON: Press Play on the video! As the music progresses, the slides will automatically advance at each character's entrance without you touching a clicker.

Jean Valjean

Hugh Jackman

The kind adoptive dad with a secret past

TIMECODE: 0:05 – 0:34

Who He Is:

A strong, gentle father who made mistakes long ago, turned his life around, and took in a lonely orphan named Cosette.

His Big Dilemma:

A ruthless police officer has discovered where he lives! Valjean must pack their bags and escape to England tomorrow to keep Cosette safe.

What To Listen For:

A slow, heavy marching beat that sounds just like a clock ticking down their final hours.

PART 2 • TIMECODE 0:35 – 0:55

Marius & Cosette: Love at First Sight & Goodbye

Marius

Eddie Redmayne

The lovestruck student

Cosette

Amanda Seyfried

The hopeful daughter

Love at First Sight:

Marius and Cosette fell instantly in love! But Cosette’s father tells her they must pack up and flee Paris tomorrow to stay safe from the police.

Marius's Big Dilemma:

Should Marius run away to follow the girl he loves, or stay behind in Paris to stand up for justice alongside his school friends?

What To Listen For:

Sweet, gentle violins as Cosette floats high above, repeating Marius's melody like a musical echo across the garden wall.

Éponine

Samantha Barks

A true friend with a broken heart, left behind in the rain

TIMECODE: 0:56 – 1:17

Her Sad Secret:

Éponine grew up poor on the city streets. She really likes Marius, but Marius only sees her as a good friend.

Her Unselfish Choice:

Even though her heart hurts watching Marius love Cosette, Éponine stays by his side to protect him no matter what.

What To Listen For:

Her voice sings the exact same tune as Marius and Cosette, but with sad, lonely words: 'One more day all on my own.'

Enjolras

Aaron Tveit

The fearless student leader wearing the bright red vest

TIMECODE: 1:18 – 1:44

Standing Up For Others:

Enjolras is a passionate young leader fighting so that poor and hungry families in Paris get treated fairly by the king.

Rallying His Friends:

He turns to Marius and asks: 'Will you join in our crusade?' Marius has to choose between his friends and his love.

What To Listen For:

Loud brass horns blast out and marching snare drums speed up the whole song with excitement!

Marius's Choice

Eddie Redmayne

Heart torn in two: Follow his sweetheart or stand with his friends?

TIMECODE: 1:45 – 2:00

The Heart vs. Duty:

Should Marius run to the harbour to catch Cosette before she sails away, or stay at the barricade with Enjolras?

Making His Decision:

Marius decides to stand shoulder-to-shoulder with his friends to fight for freedom, even if it means losing Cosette.

What To Listen For:

Notice how his sweet love melody tries to fight through the loud marching military snare drum!

Inspector Javert

Russell Crowe

The strict policeman dressed in dark blue, working as an undercover spy

TIMECODE: 2:01 – 2:22

His Secret Plan:

Javert believes rules should never be broken. He puts on a disguise to pretend he's on the students' side, but he's really spying!

Stopping the Fight:

He plans to tell the army all the students' secrets so the rebellion gets shut down before it even gets started.

What To Listen For:

A deep, stern voice that sings in short, clipped notes that sound strict and sharp like a military commander.

The Thénardiers

Sacha Baron Cohen & Helena Bonham Carter

Funny but greedy innkeepers (Éponine's sneaky parents)

TIMECODE: 2:23 – 2:48

Only Out for Themselves:

While others are fighting for freedom or love, this couple only cares about one thing: finding shiny coins and gold watches!

Their Greedy Scheme:

They plan to sneak through the street chaos picking people's pockets while nobody is paying attention.

What To Listen For:

A bouncy, comical tune that sneaks under the music like a mischievous tiptoeing cartoon character!

The Grand Finale

All 9 Main Characters & Chorus

The Polyphonic Puzzle: 9 Voices singing together into battle!

The 5 Simultaneous Vocal Lines:

Jean Valjean: Sings of praying to survive one more day on the run.
Marius & Cosette: Sing of their aching love and the pain of being separated.
Éponine: Sings sadly of walking another day all on her own.
Enjolras & Students: Shout marching chants to build the wooden barricades.
Javert & Thénardiers: Sing sharp counter-melodies of spying and looting.

What is Polyphony?

It means 'many voices at once'! 9 completely different melodies sung at the exact same moment, interlocking like colorful puzzle pieces!

The Giant Climax:

Every voice unites on the final booming shout: 'MORE!' as giant cymbals and timpani drums shake the room!

Classroom Discussion • Your Turn to Think!

What Would You Do?

Reflect on each character's tough choice on the eve of the barricade.

1

The Tough Choice

If you were Marius, would you leave Paris with your sweetheart, or stay behind to help your school friends build the barricade?

2

Musical Detective

Which character's melody stood out the most to your ears when they were all singing at the exact same time?

3

Tomorrow's Mystery

Every character sings about what 'tomorrow' will bring. Which character do you think is facing the biggest challenge?

Teacher's Reflection Prompt: Ask students to vote with thumbs up / thumbs down: If they were in Paris in 1832, would they have stood with the students or stayed safely home with their family?
